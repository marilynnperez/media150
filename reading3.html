Graphic Novel: Motherhood In Crisis

By Sarah Mirk

The four different stories given by Memunato, Heela, Kadiatu, and Tetteh were not only unfortunate to read but very eye opening. These women were faced with the fear of birthing a child whether it was their choice or not. Many people say when you start to create a family it will be a memorable time in your life. Unfortunately for these women, not only were they worried about how they were going to be able to care for their child with minimal supplies, but also feared for their lives. Fortunately within these stories they were able to find connections with centers that will help them with their situation, which gave them support and healthcare. Although these support centers were a blessing they were too affected by COVID-19 and Ebola outbreaks.
