With 'AI slop' distorting our reality, the world is sleepwalking into disaster

By Nesrine Malik

In the article “With ‘AI slop’ distorting our reality, the world is sleepwalking into disaster” by Nesrine Malik, the main argument being described is the two parallel vision words we use to consume images. These being real life images and AI generated images. It is very evident that in this current time AI has taken over many aspects in everyone's life someway or another. The problem with this is that it creates propaganda within the people when not being able to tell what is true and what is not. This being a result of the “AI-Slop”. “AI-Slop” is a phrase used to describe the events of AI generated images that are produced with no human efforts. The unfortunate part of this slop is that many people retweet, repost, and believe these clearly over exaggerated images. This is a problem because it changes the way people think and takes advantage of the people who are vulnerable and who don't know how to differentiate the differences.
